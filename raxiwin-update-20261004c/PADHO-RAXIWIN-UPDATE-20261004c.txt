==============================================

AB PERIOD BHI PICHE AUR RESULT BHI PICHE
----------------------------------------

Pichhle build me period (label) 1 piche ho gaya tha, lekin uske saamne wala
number naya wala lag raha tha (jaise "377 -> 1" jabki 1 asli 378 ka draw tha).
Ab wo bhi theek hai:

  * Har row apne hi number ka result dikhati hai (377 -> 377 ka apna draw).
  * Aur sabse naya result tab tak dikhaya nahi jata jab tak us period ka
    countdown app me khatam na ho jaye - yani poori list 1 period piche.

MAP (samajhne ke liye)
----------------------

  provider/asli haalat   : 341 chal raha hai, 340 ka draw ho chuka hai
  app ka header / bet     : 340   (1 piche)
  app ki list ka top row  : 339 + usi ka apna result (340 abhi chhupa hua hai)
  jab app ka countdown 0  : header 341 ho jata hai, list me 340 apne result
                            ke saath aa jata hai

Isliye: header = list ke top se 1 aage (jaise aapke screenshot me 334/333 tha),
bas dono numbers provider se 1 piche rehte hain.

BET PAR KYA ASAR
----------------

  - Bet usi label par lagti hai jo screen par dikhta hai (record me bhi wahi).
  - Us period ka result uske countdown 0 hone ke baad hi dikhta hai/settle
    hota hai (bet lagate hi win/loss NAHI).
  - Aur jo result settle hota hai, wahi list me us period ke saamne dikhta hai
    - dono kabhi alag nahi honge.

SWITCH (zarurat pade to)
------------------------

  table: saas_lottery_settings
     raxiwin_period_label_offset = -1  -> default: 1 period piche (ye build)
     raxiwin_period_label_offset =  0  -> live/current (jaisa purana build)

SWITCH PALATNE PAR DISPLAY AUR SETTLEMENT DONO SAATH BADALTE HAIN, isliye
mismatch nahi hoga. (Setting badalne ke baad page reload kar lein.)

EK DHYAN DENE WALI BAAT
-----------------------

Is mode me app ka feed jaan-bujh kar 1 period late hota hai. Agar koi player
kisi doosri site/feed se compare karke us period ka result pehle dekh le, to wo
apne bet ka nateeja pehle jaan sakta hai. App khud kabhi jaldi reveal nahi
karta; ye sirf bahar ki information se mumkin hai.

PEHLE SE MAUJOOD FIX (wahi hai)
-------------------------------

  - Bet par tax / service charge (stake ka 2%) record me aata hai.
  - Bet lagate hi win/loss nahi; period ke timer khatam hone par hi.
  - Chal raha period timer se pehle kabhi list me nahi aata.
