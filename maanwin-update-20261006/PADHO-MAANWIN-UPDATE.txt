ISS ZIP ME KYA FIX HUA

■ 1. PERIOD / ISSUE AB EK LINE ME HAI  ("ek period piche" wala masla)
  Pehle game jis round par bet lagata tha aur result list me sabse upar
  jo round dikhta tha, wo DONO ALAG-ALAG the. Result list hamesha ek
  period aage chalti thi, isliye lagta tha ki:
      * abhi bet lagaya -> par result kisi aur period ka dikh raha hai
      * jeet/har ka popup galat time par aata tha
      * "My Records" me bet pending hi rehta tha

  AB:
      * `Lottery/GetGameIssue` jis round ka issue deta hai (jis par aap
        bet lagate ho) -> WO HI round ab result list ki SABSE UPARI
        line me hai.
      * Ye sab jagah sync hai: game page ki result list, trend
        (GetTrendStatistics), admin ki result history, aur public JSON
        history (`/WinGo/WinGo_30S/GetHistoryIssuePage.json`).
      * Countdown / timer / period ki length me KOI BADLAV NAHI —
        WinGo 30S/1M/3M/5M, TrxWinGo, K3, 5D, MotoRace sab apne-apne
        hi interval par chalte hain.

  Test me check kiya: 8 alag-alag games ke liye
      "active issue == result list ki pehli line"  -> SAB OK

■ 2. BET LAGATE HI JEET / HAAR DECLARE  (dhaniwin wala behaviour)
  Round piche chal raha hai, isliye us round ka result pehle hi tay
  ho chuka hota hai. Isliye ab:
      * bet lagte hi server usi waqt result decide kar deta hai
      * paise usi waqt settle ho jaate hain (jeet = credit, haar = debit)
      * `GetWinLossResult` ab "pending" nahi lautaata, seedha jeet/haar
      * `My Records` me bet turant Win/Loss dikhta hai, Pending nahi

  POPUP KA TIME WAHI HAI: game client pehle se hi jeet/haar ka popup
  countdown khatam hone par dikhata hai (timer 1 sec bachne par result
  animation, uske ~2-3 sec baad popup). Is build me usko chhuna nahi
  gaya — popup ab bhi TIMER END ke baad hi aayega. Sirf result ab
  pehle hi tay rehta hai, to popup me "Pending" kabhi nahi aaega.

■ 3. ADMIN ME "IMMEDIATE SETTLEMENT" WALA CHECKBOX AB KAAM KARTA HAI
  Pehle ye checkbox bas dikhta tha — uska koi asar nahi tha.
  Ab ye sach me instant jeet/haar ko ON/OFF karta hai:
      * ticked  -> bet lagte hi result (DEFAULT: ON)
      * untick  -> purana behaviour (result round khatam hone par)
  Admin > WinGo panel > "WinGo / lottery engine settings" me hai.
  Save karne ke baad Ctrl+F5 kar lena.

■ 4. ADMIN KE FORCE WIN / FORCE LOSE AB BHI KAAM KARTE HAIN
  Instant result ke saath ek dikkat thi: result list pehle ban jaati
  thi, uske baad bet aata tha — to admin ka Force Win/Lose miss ho
  jaata. Isko theek kiya: settle karte waqt bhi force mode check hota
  hai, to Force Win -> hamesha jeet, Force Lose -> hamesha haar.
  Test: dono mode me check kiya -> OK

■ 5. KOI AUR CHEEZ NAHI CHHERI

  wager, gift code, support ticket — kuch nahi chhera.
