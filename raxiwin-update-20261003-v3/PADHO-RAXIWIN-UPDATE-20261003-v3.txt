V3 ME KYA NAYA (v2 ke baad) - SABSE ZAROORI
-------------------------------------------

Aapne bataya tha: "upar 91 period dikh raha hai lekin bet 92 par lag gayi,
1 period piche hai to bet bhi piche hi rehni chahiye".

- v2 me piche wale label ko aage (running period par) book kiya jata tha,
  isliye screen par 91 aur bet 92 wala farak bacha hua tha.
- Ab v3 me: SCREEN PAR JO PERIOD DIKHTA HAI, BET THEEK USI PAR LAGTI HAI.
  Bet ko kabhi aage nahi badhaya jata. Display ka number = bet ka number.
- Aur wo bet us period ka TIMER 0 hone ke baad hi settle hoti hai
  (naya column saas_lottery_bets.settle_after). Isliye:
     * bet lagate hi win/loss kabhi nahi,
     * result sirf us period ke timer khatam hone ke baad hi milta hai.

Purana (cached) client ho ya naya client - dono me bet apne displayed period
par hi rehti hai, kyunki ye fix ab SERVER (engine) me hai, browser JS par
nirbhar nahi.


KYA FIX HUA
-----------

1) BET KA TAX / SERVICE CHARGE  ("bet me tax wagera fix")
   - Pehle bet record me tax/service charge hamesha 0.00 aata tha, isliye
     "amount after tax" = poori stake dikhti thi.
   - Ab har bet par tax stake ke saath hi save hota hai:
        tax = stake * 2%   (setting key: lottery_payout_tax_percent)
   - Record / API me ye fields bhar ke jaate hain:
        realAmount, realBettingAmount, fee, serviceCharge, tax, taxAmount,
        taxRate
     Isliye My Record / bet history screen me tax wali line dikhegi.
   - Purane bets ka tax bhi pehli request par ek baar automatically
     backfill ho jata hai (koi SQL chalane ki zarurat nahi).
   - Winning payout par se tax pehle ki tarah hi (2% default) katta hai;
     tax % aap chahein to badal sakte hain (neeche SETTINGS dekhein).

2) ISSUE KA NUMBER aur BET ka PERIOD - dono ab EK HI
   ("91 dikha, bet 92 par lag gayi")
   - Server ab client ke bheje hue displayed label ko hi apna period maanta
     hai (running period par aage nahi khiskata, aur reject bhi nahi karta).
   - Server khud verify karta hai ki label current ya just-previous canonical
     period hai; isse bahar ka label fail ho jata hai
     ("Betting has stopped for the current period").
   - History / result panel me bhi wahi labels bina shift ke dikhte hain,
     aur jo period chal raha hai wo timer khatam hone se pehle list me
     nahi aata.

3) BET LAGATE HI WIN/LOSS NEHI - TIMER KHATAM HONE KA WAIT
   ("bet lagate hi win ho jaa rha h")
   - Pehle: agar result pehle se save tha to bet turant usi saved result
     se settle kar diya jata tha -> bet lagate hi win/loss.
   - Ab: har nayi bet ke saath us period ka end-time save hota hai, aur
     settlement us end-time ke baad hi hoti hai (settle_after).
   - Timer khatam hone ke baad bhi result pending nahi atakta:
        * result save hote hi pending bets settle ho jaate hain,
        * closing draw ka thoda (max ~3 second) wait hota hai, taaki round
          khatam hone ke turant baad wale poll me hi won/lost + amount
          mil jaye (popup aa jaye),
        * cached client ka piche wala label ho to bhi engine usi bet ka
          result dhoondh kar deta hai (issue +/-1 tak safe).

4) HISTORY / RESULT PANEL
   - Result list ke label pehle shift kiye jaate the; ab labels bina shift
     ke, sahi period par dikhte hain (draw-live-v4/index.php).
   - Ek period ka result sirf uske timer khatam hone ke baad hi public
     history me aata hai.

UPLOAD KAISE KARNA HAI (cPanel)
-------------------------------

1. cPanel > File Manager kholein.
2. Site ke root folder me jayein (jahan index.html aur saas_lottery folder hai).
3. Is ZIP ko wahi upload karein.
4. ZIP par right click > Extract > Overwrite / Replace "Yes" karein.
   (ZIP ke andar saas_lottery/..., draw-live-v4/... aur assets/js/... path
   already sahi hai, isliye files apni jagah chali jayengi. Koi folder
   manually move karne ki zarurat nahi.)
5. Page ko Ctrl+F5 (hard refresh) kar lein - purani JS cache se hata dein.
   Mobile app / PWA me ho to cache clear karke dobara kholein.
6. Test:
   - WinGo khol kar dekhein: upar jo issue number dikh raha ho, bet lagane
     ke baad My Record / win-loss me wahi number aana chahiye.
   - Ek WinGo bet lagayein -> bet lagte hi winner popup NAHI aana chahiye;
     jo period screen par chal raha hai uska timer 0 hone ke baad hi
     win/loss aaye.
   - My Record me us bet row me tax / service charge (fee) 0.00 ki jagah
     2% dikhna chahiye.


SETTINGS (optional - table: saas_lottery_settings)
--------------------------------------------------

  lottery_payout_tax_percent = 2.00   -> tax percentage (default 2)
  accept_previous_period_bets = 1     -> 1 (default): cached client ka
                                         piche wala label bhi accept hota
                                         hai aur USI (displayed) period par
                                         book hota hai.
                                         0 karne par sirf exactly current
                                         period ka label accept hota hai,
                                         baaki par "Betting has stopped".

Dono soorat me bet apne period ke timer khatam hone se pehle settle NAHI
hoti aur display ka issue number = bet ka issue number rehta hai.
