      ₹4 dikhata tha, chahe uske paas 44,982 ho.

      block kar raha tha. Ye 4.11 sach me koi wager nahi hai — purane build
      ka seedha hua dummy number hai.

  * api/_bootstrap.php me "live endpoints" ki ek list banayi gayi — recharge,
    withdraw, user info, wallet, lottery/getbalance, recharge/withdraw
    history, gift-code, support ticket aur work-order endpoints.
    In sab ka jawab ab SIRF database se aata hai — koi saved snapshot nahi.
  * api_get_override() ab in endpoints ke liye snapshot ko saaf saaf
    REFUSE karta hai (pehle router to rok deta tha, par endpoint ki apni
    file phir bhi snapshot serve kar deti thi — asli leak wahi thi).
  * 4 endpoints ki files (Recharge/GetRechargeBasicInfo, User/GetUserInfo,

    Server isko 0 hi rakhta hai, isliye screen par wager line dikhegi hi
    nahi aur withdraw button chalta hai.
  * Ek baar chalne wala auto-cleanup (koi manual DB kaam nahi):
      - amount_coding ko 0 kar diya (jo 4.11 pada tha).
      - in endpoints ke saved snapshots ko hamesha ke liye OFF kar diya.
      - agar kisi user ke wallet/game do column me different paise hain to
        wo ek hi balance me merge ho jaate hain (paisa na kam, na zyada).
