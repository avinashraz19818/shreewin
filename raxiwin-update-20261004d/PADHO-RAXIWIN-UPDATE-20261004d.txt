AB BET RECORD KA TIME BHI 1 PERIOD PICHE
-----------------------------------------

Screenshot me dikh raha tha: period label 1 piche hai (jaise 385), lekin uske
saamne wala time asli placement time hai (11:55:15) - jo provider ke period ka
waqt hai. Ab wo time bhi 1 period piche ho jayega:

      period 385  ->  pehle 2026-10-04 11:55:15 dikhta tha
                  ->  ab   2026-10-04 11:54:15 dikhega

Yahi 30sec / 3min / 5min games me bhi hoga (30s ka 30 second piche, 3M ka 3
minute piche, 5M ka 5 minute piche) - yani poora record period ke sath match
karega.

Isse pehle jo bana tha (wahi is build me bhi hai):
  * header / bet / record ka period label 1 piche
  * history me har row apne hi number ka result, aur sabse naya result 1 period
    baad (jab uska countdown app me khatam ho)
  * bet ke period ka timer khatam hone se pehle win/loss nahi
  * bet par tax / service charge (stake ka 2%)

KAHAN-KAHAN LAGTA HAI

------------------------

  table: saas_lottery_settings
     raxiwin_period_label_offset = -1  -> default: 1 period piche

     raxiwin_period_label_offset =  0  -> live/current (time bhi asli rehta hai)
