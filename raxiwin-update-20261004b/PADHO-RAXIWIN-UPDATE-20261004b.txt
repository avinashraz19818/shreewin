==============================================

POORI APP 1 PERIOD PICHE
------------------------

Aapne kaha: "1 period piche nhi chl rha h, final file do jisme sab proper piche
ho jaye". Ab poora WinGo screen, bet, record, history aur result - SAB ek hi
(lagging) label par chalte hain, exactly jaise pehle chalta tha.

MAP (samajhne ke liye)
----------------------

  provider ki asli running period : 341  (jaise BDG WIN me dikhta hai)
  app par dikhta hai               : 340  (1 piche)  <- header period
  app ka countdown                 : 341 ke window ka hi rehta hai (wahi waqt)

  Jab wo countdown 0 hota hai:
     - app ka period 341 ho jata hai,
     - aur 340 ka RESULT history me aa jata hai (jo draw hua).

  Matlab: har period apne puraane number se hi dikhta hai, aur uska result
  uske countdown khatam hone par hi aata hai - bilkul consistent.

KYA-KYA PICHE SHIFT HUA (aur kahin mismatch nahi rahega)
--------------------------------------------------------

1) HEADER (running period)   : provider - 1
2) BET                       : jo label screen par (340) usi par bet banti hai,
                               record me bhi 340 hi jata hai.
3) WIN/LOSS                  : 340 bet ka result 341 wale draw se banta hai,
                               aur wo draw tab aata hai jab 340 ka timer khatam
                               ho (bet lagate hi win/loss NAHI).
4) HISTORY / RESULT LIST     : sab rows ka label 1 piche, number/colour wahi
                               jo asli draw hua. Chal raha period timer khatam
                               hone se pehle list me nahi aata.
5) RECORD (My Record)        : wahi label jo screen par tha.

Histoy me "header = top row + 1" hi rahega (jaise aapke screenshot me 334/333
tha) - bas ab wo dono numbers provider se 1 piche honge.

SWITCH (zarurat pade to)
------------------------

  table: saas_lottery_settings
     raxiwin_period_label_offset = -1   -> default: 1 period piche (ye build)
     raxiwin_period_label_offset =  0   -> canonical/current (jaisa purane
                                           20261004 build me tha)

  Display aur settlement DONO isi ek setting se chalta hai, isliye switch karne
  par bhi display = bet = result rehta hai. Koi code change nahi.
