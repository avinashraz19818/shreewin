WINGO UI UPDATE — 2026-09-14
===========================

Do chhote changes (sirf WinGo page):

1. "Follow Strategy" tab hata diya
   ---------------------------------------------------------------
   WinGo page par "Game history | Chart | Follow Strategy" wale nav me se
   Follow Strategy tab ab kahin nahi aayega.

   Kaise: client ye tab sirf tab dikhata hai jab uska `isOpenFollow` flag
   true ho. Wo flag do jagah se aata hai, aur dono jagah band kar diya gaya:
     - server: `api-live-v4/Lottery/index.php` (GetUserInfo) me
       'isOpenFollow' => false
     - browser: `same-origin-lottery.js` me localStorage ki value false par
       pin kar di, taaki purane login/session me pada hua `true` bhi tab ko
       wapas on na kar sake.

   Wapas chalu karna ho to: `api-live-v4/Lottery/index.php` me
   'isOpenFollow'=>false  ->  'isOpenFollow'=>app_setting_bool('follow_strategy_enabled',true)
   kar do aur `same-origin-lottery.js` me isOpenFollow wali 3 lines hata do.

2. Multiplier row: sirf X1 X5 X10 X20 X50 X100
   ---------------------------------------------------------------
   Pehle:  Random  X1 X2 X3 X4 X5 X10 X20 X50 X100
   Ab:     Random  X1 X5 X10 X20 X50 X100

   (Random wahi hai jo pehle tha — wo multiplier value nahi, random multiple
    chunne wala button hai. Uske liye kuch nahi chheda. Hatana ho to bata dena.)

   Kaise: multiplier list client ko server se milti hai —
   `saas_lottery/bootstrap_live_v4.php` ke `sl_game_info()` se. Wahan WinGo
   family ke liye array(1,5,10,20,50,100) kar diya. Baaki lottery games
   (TrxWinGo, K3, D5, MotoRace) ki list waise hi rakhi hai jaisi thi:
   array(1,2,3,4,5,10,20,50,100). Un me bhi yehi list chahiye to bata dena —
   1 line ka change hai.
