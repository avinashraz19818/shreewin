KYA FIX HUA
-----------

1) BET KA TAX / SERVICE CHARGE  ("bet me tax wagera fix")
   - Pehle bet record me tax/service charge hamesha 0.00 aata tha, isliye
     "amount after tax" = poori stake dikhti thi.
   - Ab har bet par tax stake ke saath hi save hota hai:
        tax = stake * 2%   (setting key: lottery_payout_tax_percent)
   - Record / API me ye fields bhar ke jaate hain:
        realAmount, realBettingAmount, fee, serviceCharge, tax, taxAmount,
        taxRate
     Isliye My Record / bet history screen me tax wali line dikhegi.
   - Purane bets ka tax bhi pehli request par ek baar automatically
     backfill ho jata hai (koi SQL chalane ki zarurat nahi).
   - Winning payout par se tax pehle ki tarah hi (2% default) katta hai;
     tax % aap chahein to badal sakte hain (neeche SETTINGS dekhein).

2) WIN / LOSS jo 1 PERIOD PICHE CHAL RAHA THA
   - Client apna issue label ek period piche bhejta hai (ye app ka wahi
     purana behaviour hai, wo humne nahi chheda).
   - Ab us period ka RESULT PEHLE SE SAVE HO to bet reject nahi hota:
        * bet turant accept hota hai,
        * usi saved result se won/lost turant lag jata hai aur jeet ki
          raqam wallet me credit ho jati hai.
     Yaani "jo result aa chuka hai, uspe bet lage to win/loss ready".
   - GetWinLossResult ab kabhi bet ko pending chhod kar "null" nahi deta:
        * result save hote hi pending bets settle kar deta hai,
        * closing draw ka thoda (max ~4 second) wait karta hai, taaki
          round khatam hone ke turant baad wale ek hi poll me hi
          won/lost + amount mil jaye (popup aa jaye).
   - Pehle se jitni bets pending atki hui hain, unka bhi settlement ho jata
     hai jab unka result table me aa jata hai.
   - Result display / labels / win-luck list ka format jaisa chal raha tha
     waisa hi hai, usme koi change nahi kiya.

UPLOAD KAISE KARNA HAI (cPanel)
-------------------------------

1. cPanel > File Manager kholein.
2. Site ke root folder me jayein (jahan index.html aur saas_lottery folder hai).
3. Is ZIP ko wahi upload karein.
4. ZIP par right click > Extract > Overwrite / Replace "Yes" karein.
   (ZIP ke andar saas_lottery/... path already sahi hai, isliye files apni
   jagah chali jayengi. Koi folder manually move karne ki zarurat nahi.)
5. Bas ho gaya. Page ko Ctrl+F5 (hard refresh) kar lein.
6. Test:
   - Ek WinGo bet lagayein -> My Record me us bet row me tax / service charge
     (fee) 0.00 ki jagah 2% dikhna chahiye.
   - Round khatam hone ke baad winner popup me win/loss aur amount aa jana
     chahiye (pehle kai baar kuch nahi aata tha).
   - Jo bet pichle (already nikle hue) period par lag jaye, us par bhi
     turant win/loss lag jayega.


SETTINGS (optional - table: saas_lottery_settings)
--------------------------------------------------

  lottery_payout_tax_percent = 2.00   -> tax percentage (default 2)
  accept_previous_period_bets = 1     -> 1 (default) pe lagging period ka bet
                                         accept + turant settle hota hai.
                                         0 karne par aisa bet dobara reject
                                         hoga (sirf current period ke bet
                                         chalenge).

Dhyan rakhein: lagging (1 period piche) bet already nikle result par lagta
hai, isliye uska win/loss turant ready hota hai. Agar aap nahi chahte ki
aisi bets lagein, to accept_previous_period_bets = 0 kar dein.
